# Security

Please don't open a public issue for a security problem. Email **hello@dasig.org** with "Security" in the subject: what you found, how to reproduce it, and what it affects. We reply within 3 working days and credit reporters who want it.

Records in BIX can describe volunteers, children and families. Reports about privacy (anything that shows a person, a photo or a group that shouldn't be visible) count as security reports.
